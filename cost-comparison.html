How the call-forwarding works (all setups)
The barber shop's existing number call-forwards to a Twilio number → Twilio routes via SIP trunk to the AI agent (VAPI, LiveKit, or Dograh). Twilio cost is identical across all three setups: $0.0085/min inbound + $1.15/month per number. For VAPI the SIP destination is VAPI's endpoint; for LiveKit it's LiveKit Cloud's SIP inbound; for Dograh it's Dograh's built-in Twilio handler.

Why VAPI costs 6× more: VAPI charges $0.05/min platform fee on top of every AI cost. On top of that they mark up GPT-4.1 from ~$0.009/min (direct OpenAI) to $0.07/min — a 7.5× LLM markup. Deepgram goes from $0.0059/min direct to $0.01/min on VAPI. All these markups stack up to $0.1585/min total vs ~$0.023/min self-hosted.
