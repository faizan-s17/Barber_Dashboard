# Your Dashboard — A Quick Guide

For the shop. No technical knowledge needed. If something looks wrong, the last section
tells you who to call.

---

## The pages

**Overview** — today at a glance: who's coming in, expected takings, your team and menu.

**Calendar** — every booking. Click one to see the full details including the
**booking reference**. You can add a booking by hand here, and export the day or month
to a spreadsheet or PDF.

**Clients** — everyone who's ever booked, how many visits, when they're next in.
You can search by name, phone, email, **or booking reference** — so when someone rings
and reads out their reference, type it straight into the search box.

**Waiting List** — people waiting for a slot to free up. See §"When someone cancels".

**Calls** — a log of calls the AI has handled.

**Usage** — how many minutes and calls you've used this month against your plan.

**Settings** — shop details, team, who does what, opening hours, your plan.

## Everyday jobs

### Add or remove a barber
Settings → Barbers. Removing someone doesn't delete their past bookings.

### Change opening hours
Settings → Opening Hours. The AI picks this up straight away — no need to tell anyone.

### Block a holiday or a day off
Settings → Barbers, or add the time off directly. The AI will stop offering that barber
for those dates immediately.

### Change a price or add a service
Settings → Shop Info and the Services page. Include how long it takes **and** the
clean-up time you need afterwards — the AI uses both to work out what fits.

### Say who does what
**Settings → Who Does What.** This is the one people forget. If Tony doesn't do colours,
untick it — otherwise the AI can offer Tony to someone asking for a colour.

If you see an orange warning saying everyone is marked as doing everything, that's the
setup default and it needs correcting.

## Booking references

Every booking gets a six-character reference like **H4K92R**.

- The AI reads it out on the phone and it appears on the confirmation
- It never contains the letters **I, L, O or U**, or the digits **0 or 1** — so if a
  customer says one of those, they've misheard. Ask them to read it again.
- A customer who's lost theirs can still cancel — the AI will find them by phone number
  or by name and date. Never turn someone away over a lost reference.

## When someone cancels

The freed slot is offered automatically to people on the waiting list. Several get told at
once and **the first to reply gets it** — so you may see it fill within minutes.

You don't need to do anything. The Waiting List page shows live offers counting down.

## If the AI does something odd

1. **Check the Calendar first.** Nine times out of ten the booking is there and the
   customer just didn't hear the confirmation.
2. **Check AI Health.** It shows whether the system is responding.
3. **Check Usage** — if you've run out of minutes, calls may not be handled.

Things that are working as intended, not faults:
- The AI won't take card payments over the phone
- It won't give discounts or change prices
- It won't book a slot that doesn't fit the service length plus clean-up time
- It offers a different barber when the one asked for is busy — you can change the order
  it does this in

## Who to call

**SkyWeb** — anything about the phone assistant, the dashboard, or bookings that look wrong.

Have ready: the **booking reference**, roughly **when the call happened**, and **what the
customer says they were told**. Those three things make it quick to find.

**Do not** give out your dashboard login. Each staff member gets their own.
